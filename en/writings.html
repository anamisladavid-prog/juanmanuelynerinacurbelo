Leadership School

Liderar en Plenitud

Pastors Juan Manuel and Nerina Curbelo

Leadership that transforms others begins long before a platform: it is born in character, strengthened in God’s presence, and demonstrated through service.

Liderar en Plenitud is a Spanish-language training guide for those who want to approach ministry with depth, maturity, and purpose. Juan Manuel and Nerina Curbelo bring together biblical principles, pastoral experience, and practical tools to help readers understand their identity, nurture their spiritual life, build healthy teams, and lead others with integrity. Across twenty lessons, the book invites readers to move beyond holding a position and become leaders who inspire, support, and transform.
