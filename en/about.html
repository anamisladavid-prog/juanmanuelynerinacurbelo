Our story

Two lives united by faith and purpose.

A story built on family, service, and the conviction that every person can live in the fullness of their calling.

Juan Manuel & Nerina Curbelo

A life devoted to developing people, strengthening families, and equipping leaders.

Their story brings together pastoral ministry, teaching, and a deep conviction: every person can experience God’s restoration and fully live out their purpose.

Juan Manuel and Nerina Curbelo are the senior pastors of Iglesia Plenitud de Vida in Mendoza, Argentina, and directors of Plenitud de Vida Internacional Inc., a foundation based in Puerto Rico.

Alongside their pastoral work, they lead an extensive teaching ministry through conferences, workshops, and training programs for educational institutions, businesses, and churches.

Both hold degrees and serve as professors in Theology and Philosophy. They are also trained family counselors and graduates of the International Baptist Theological Seminary of Buenos Aires. As speakers and authors, they have devoted their lives to developing people, strengthening families, and equipping leaders to live out and fulfill God’s purpose.

With more than two decades in ministry, 30 years of marriage, and a network of churches in Argentina and Paraguay, they have built a legacy marked by faithful teaching of God’s Word, healthy leadership, and a passion for the holistic growth of the Church. Through books, conferences, training programs, and podcasts, they share biblical principles and practical tools that inspire a mature, transformative faith relevant to the challenges of our time.

Together they have raised four children: Emanuel, married to Ana Cristina; Nicolás, married to Florencia; Miqueas; and Gianella Abigail. They also cherish the joy of being grandparents to Esteban, the son of Emanuel and Ana Cristina, and to Gracia and Mateo, the children of Nicolás and Florencia. Outside ministry, they deeply value family life and serving God together.

Their greatest desire is for every resource they create to draw people closer to Christ, strengthen their character, and inspire them to live with conviction, hope, and purpose. They believe God has called them to help every person and family experience the healing, restoration, and transformation that only He can provide, aligning their lives with His original purpose for them.

“As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord.”
